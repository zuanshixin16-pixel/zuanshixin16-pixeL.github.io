About Me

Trust, built on two disciplines.

Before the mat, there was the clinic. A career in nephrology shaped a way of seeing the body as a system of channels, fluids, and rhythms — the same lens that later found its match in the twelve meridians of qigong.

  • MDNephrologist by training, specialist in the body's internal rivers and balance.
  • 16+ YearsTeaching qigong and yoga across studios, clinics, and online.
  • E-RYT 500Yoga Alliance certified Experienced Registered Yoga Teacher.
  • YACEPYoga Alliance Continuing Education Provider.
“My own thyroid healing is what turned meridian theory from an academic interest into a daily practice — and eventually, into a way of teaching.”

Teaching philosophy: meet the body where it is. Every posture and breath is offered as an adjustable practice — for a teacher trainee, a senior student, or someone managing a chronic condition — never a fixed shape to force yourself into.
